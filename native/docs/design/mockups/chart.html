Chart view: quick mockups

The chord chart (D7, ships in 2.1) inside the Perform v6 layout. You choose where it goes, per device: on the stage, while the instruments shrink to live mini faders (⇥ Sidebar moves it), or as a tab in the right sidebar (⤢ Stage moves it back). The sidebar is resizable: drag its left edge (try it in the sidebar tabs); dragging it very narrow folds it to the icon rail. Chords follow the song's key: this chart was written in G and the song is in C, so it shows in C. Heard shows the key the band hears, Hands the key your hands play when Easy Transpose is on. Text size is one tap away: 100% is the default size; the small and big A buttons step 50% · 60% · 75% · 90% · 100% · 110% · 125% · 150% · 175% · 200%, tapping the % goes back to 100%, Fit picks the biggest size where the longest line still fits, and pinch, ⌘+ / ⌘− or ⌘-scroll work on the chart itself. Each device remembers its own size. The gold line is where auto-scroll reads; the section buttons jump. The song and words are made up for the mockup. Nothing is wired up.
