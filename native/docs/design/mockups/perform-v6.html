Perform v6: the strips are the main screen

Quick HTML mockups in the app's Sanctuary v2 colours and fonts; nothing is wired up. The instrument strips fill the screen, as in Sunday Keys, with today's Wheel column and a Quick button in a left sidebar (Quick opens over the strips, like today's sheet, so nothing moves while you play), and a sidebar on the right. The sidebar collapses to a thin rail (⟩), shows one module at a time as tabs, drag its left edge to make it wider or narrower, and ⋯ picks and orders which modules it holds: Drone, Setlist, Notes, Tone or Snapshots. The bottom keyboard is today's, with a range bar per instrument; drag either end of a colour to set which keys that instrument plays (try it). Transpose and Space moved into the song bar and snapshots got their own row (⌃ folds it into a chip in the song bar, ▾ brings it back), so the strips get the room. Colours go by position, not by kind of sound. With 6 or more instruments the strips keep the 5-across width and the row scrolls sideways; the coloured pills above it show every instrument and which ones are on screen (tap one to jump to it). In this page you can scroll the 7-instrument views.
