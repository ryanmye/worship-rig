## Code read

- **Allocator** (`voice_alloc.h`): matches voice.js `makeRoom` exactly:
  - The victim is the earliest released voice whose release happened by `when`, else the oldest started voice.
  - Ties go to insertion order. Killed voices do not count.
  - Every loop is bounded by 160 slots; the worst case is lowering the cap from 128 to 32, about 31k compares.
  - No allocation and no locks.
- **Kernels**:
  - `VoiceFilter` follows `biquad_filter_handler.cc`: k-rate frequency/detune, first frame of the detune inputs, Q
    per frame only while automated.
  - `ConstPan` uses `fdlibm` cos/sin as in `stereo_panner.cc`.
  - Tables are built in JS double arithmetic through `rig::js`.
  - `oscProcess` makes the two extra `FinalValue()` calls that Chromium's `OscillatorHandler::Process` makes per
    quantum: in `CalculateSampleAccuratePhaseIncrements`, in `Process`, and in `ProcessKRate`. This is backed by
    `warm-params`/`strings-vib`, where detune `rampTo` would fail otherwise.
- **Thread use**:
  - The wavebank mutex is taken only on the control thread, in constructors and in `onRateChanged`.
  - `SetWaves` swaps a `shared_ptr` by pointer, with no refcount traffic on the audio thread, and returns the old
    bank through `Owned`/Garbage.
  - Function-local statics are initialised on the control thread by the constructors, except `butter4Q` for the
    strings/drone voices. Its first use is a guard-variable init with no allocation, which is acceptable.
- **Denormals**: every render path runs inside `rt::RenderScope` (FTZ/DAZ).
- **Bounds**: `params_[16]` against a maximum of 10 params; source and pitched arrays are sized for the largest
  recipe. No overrun was found, and ASan is clean.
- **Smoothing vs contract and JS**:
  - `setBend`: `rampTo` 8 ms.
  - Params: `rampTo` default τ, read-at-note-on params ignored.
  - Morph: per patch, as synth.js.
  - Legato: `inst.params.glide ?? 0.03`, as JS. The command's `glide` is ignored, as in audio.js `_monoOn`.
  - Release override: instrument-wide, read at release.

## Fixed in this review

| id | sev | file(s) | change |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | MEDIUM | `src/inst/synth.{h,cpp}`, `tests/test_synth.cpp` | **Stale "currentTime" after a rate change.** `Graph::adoptRate` rescales the frame grid, calls `setSampleRate`, then applies the quantum's commands before `process()`. `atTime()` divided the old-rate `nextFrame_` by the new rate. On a downward change, that quantum's note-ons, params and bends were clamped into the future by the ratio: 48 → 44.1 kHz at t = 1000 s gives an 88 s delay. Now `setSampleRate` rescales `nextFrame_` the way the graph does. The new test "rate change down (96 -> 48 kHz)" fails without the fix (silence) and passes with it. |
| F2 | LOW | `src/inst/synth.{h,cpp}`, `tests/test_synth.cpp` | **Panic killed a note-on of the same quantum.** `kill()` set a flag that `process()` applied to every voice, including one started after the Panic in the same quantum. The Model sends `Op::Panic` today, until T14. Now `kill()` records the newest construction seq and only older voices die. This is tested. |
| V1 | test gap | `tools/golden/vectors/dump-synth.mjs`, `data/synth.txt`, `tests/test_synth_vectors.cpp` | Five Chromium cases no fixture covered, now permanent: `warm-gap` (filter LFO across a gap with no voices, 2.1e-6), `glass-gap` (the chorus LFO is pulled only while a side oscillator plays, 1.4e-6), `warm-steal-rel` (cap 4, steal of released voices by release time, 5.7e-6), `warm-48k` (1.4e-6), `keys-48k` (7.8e-5, the soft-keys FM blocker). Per-case `sr` added. The test reads `T11_VECTORS=<file>` for ad-hoc probes. The data was regenerated in Chromium 157.0.8103.78, and the old 10 cases moved by at most 5.3e-6 (drone-osc). That is Chromium's own run-to-run input-sum order. |

## Open items

| id | sev | finding | exact change / owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| O1 | MEDIUM | **First-touch page faults on the audio thread at note-on.** The pool's raw block is 19.2 MB per instrument; glibc serves it with a fresh `mmap`, so every new instrument (every song switch) starts with untouched pages. Measured (scratch bench, g++ Release): a warm-pad note-on costs **56 µs on an untouched slot vs 11 µs on a reused one**. A 16-note first chord costs 0.9 ms inside one quantum. With 64 voices sounding (≈ 0.58 ms) that exceeds the 1.33 ms budget of a 64-frame buffer (D2), and the iPad's fault cost is higher. | T11: add `VoicePool::prefault(unsigned slots)` (memset `kStride * slots` bytes) and call it from the `SynthInstrument` constructor for the first 16 slots (1.9 MB resident per synth instrument). `freeSlot()` already reuses the lowest index, so normal playing stays inside the prefaulted range. T12 does the same for the sampler. The resident cost (16 slots × 1.9 MB) goes to ARCHITECTURE §7.1. Not applied here because it is a memory-budget decision (D3, iPad). |
| O2 | MEDIUM (T11a) | The 3-call `FinalValue` behaviour is compensated in T11's `oscProcess`, not in `wa::Oscillator`. Every other oscillator user (T05 chorus LFO `rampTo` on frequency, T15 synth-extra, T16 drone) runs one step per quantum where Chromium runs three, whenever frequency/detune follow a `setTarget` without sample-accurate values. | T11a owner: move the two extra `processKRate()` pairs into `wa::Oscillator::process` (the condition is in `oscProcess`). `oscFinalValueCalls()` probes the oscillator at startup and drops T11's compensation by itself. Then re-check T05 `chorus-fast-deep-noise`. |
| O3 | LOW | **Rate change: retiring synth instruments keep the old-rate tables.** `onRateChanged` re-points only `Inst0..15` with a synth.js id, so a retiring tail plays at the wrong pitch (×new/old) until it ends. Every instrument also plays with old tables between `adoptRate` and the arrival of `SetWaves` (one control round trip). | T13/T11: the Model has to keep the retiring slot's `instId` (or expose it), so `onRateChanged` can loop `retireInstSlot(j)`. Do not send `SetWaves` to synth-extra units, which share kind `inst.synth`. |
| O4 | LOW | Parity path through the platform libm: `VoiceFilter` uses `std::exp2(float)`, as T11a's oscillator does. It equals Chromium only where the libm equals glibc's `exp2f`; Apple libm may differ by 1 ulp. | Orchestrator/T01: a `rig::` port of glibc `exp2f` for all three uses (`dsp/oscillator.cpp` ×2, `inst/synth_dsp.cpp`), then check on the Mac. |
| O5 | LOW | Bounded side tables drop silently: `pendingEnded_` (16 evicted-voice `VoiceEnded` events per quantum) and `garbage_` (8 payloads, a leak if exceeded). Eviction needs all 160 slots in use, so this is rare. | T11: count the drops into a stat, or size `pendingEnded_` to the spare count (32). |
| O6 | INFO | `WaveBank::trim()` has no caller (memory pressure), and bank memory is not reported. | T08/T11 when the memory-pressure path is wired. |
| O7 | INFO | Not re-measured here: ui-core 25/76 via the devhost; TSan (no threading change, which I agree with); the CPU numbers on the Mac and iPad. | T09 re-run; Mac list. |
