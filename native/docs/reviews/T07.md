# T07 review (skeptic): click & MIDI

Verdict: **PARTIAL**. Every measurable claim in the builder's report reproduced in `native/core/build-t07-review`
(g++ Release), `build-t07-review-clang` (clang Release) and `build-t07-review-asan` (g++ ASan+UBSan). Two
divergences from the JS engine were found in T07's own files and are fixed (F1, F2). Two parity gaps remain, and both
need files T07 does not own: the recorder and the meters hear the 'main' click (O1), and `newBar` is dropped on a
song switch (O2). The ctest is not green until the orchestrator applies the `test_golden.cpp` change (O3).

## Measured

The goldens are Chromium 157.0.8103.78 renders. Their sha256 matches `manifest.json`. Both fixtures were rendered
with `rig_render` and compared with `tools/golden/compare.mjs`, before and after the fixes, on g++, clang and ASan.
The sample-level check is an independent numpy comparison.

| fixture | tier | max abs diff | band dB | env corr | result | golden silent? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| click-120-44 | exact | 0 | 0.00 | 1.0000 | PASS, bit-exact (g++, clang, ASan) | no: peak 0.5, one onset at 0.1000 s, 1763 non-zero samples |
| click-90-68-countin | exact | 0 | 0.00 | 1.0000 | PASS, bit-exact (g++, clang, ASan) | no: peak 0.3, six onsets 0.1000 … 1.2111 s (one count-in bar of 6/8 eighths, then quiet) |

- **"The click-120-44 golden is a single click": confirmed.** The manifest's `resolved.stats.click` for this fixture
  shows `ticks: 1, pumps: 1, running: false`. page.js issues `clickStop({when: 2.1})` before rendering starts, which
  cancels the pump. The fixture therefore checks one accent at level 1 (the 10 ms `rampTo` from t = 0), the click
  table and the sub-sample `start()`. It checks no click track. The count-in fixture covers fractional start
  frames (0.3222 s and later), which are bit-exact.
- **Neither fixture exercises** MIDI clock, `record`, `aux`, a level change while running, or a tempo or signature
  change. These are covered only by the metronome vectors, and those use a fake Host, so the service glue
  (`ClickServiceImpl::onSongState`) is not checked against the JS. That is how O2 went unnoticed.
- **Vectors.** `dump-click.mjs` and `dump-midi.mjs` were re-run. They load the real `metronome.js`, `midi.js` and
  `controller.js` and produce output byte-identical to the committed data: 14 cases, 131 ticks, 2,583 clock lines.
- **ctest** (44 tests, soak excluded), after the fixes:
  - g++: 42/44. clang: 42/44. ASan+UBSan: 42/44, with no sanitizer reports. The two failures are the same on all
    three builds: `unit.clock` and `unit.golden-analytic`.
  - `rt.realtime` and `unit.sources` passed under ASan on this run, so the builder's two extra ASan failures look
    like load flakes.
  - `unit.click` + `unit.midi`: 14 cases, 33,011 assertions, green on all three builds.
- **`unit.clock` is not T07's.** The note onset is late by exactly 6.0 ms at both rates: 576 frames at 96 kHz and 192
  frames at 32 kHz. That is the master-chain lookahead (T03), and the test's expectation predates it. The test never
  starts the click.
- **CPU per 128-frame quantum** (fastest of 20 runs):

  | build | idle | two clicks sounding |
  |---|---|---|
  | g++ | 46 ns | 325 ns |
  | clang | 35 ns | 136 ns |

  Two sounding clicks on g++ take 0.012 % of a core at 48 kHz. The builder's "0.12 %" is the ASan figure.
- **Real-time safety** (click unit):
  - Fixed pool of 32 `wa::BufferSource` voices. `wa::Param` has a fixed capacity of 64, so voice assignment
    allocates nothing.
  - The click tables are built once in the constructor, on the control thread.
  - No locks, and every loop is bounded (32 voices × 128 frames).
  - The output is zeroed every quantum. FTZ/DAZ come from the graph.
  - The allocation trap is on in the unit tests and in the golden path.
  - The parser, router, port table and services run on the control thread.
- **Ports against their sources:**
  - `clickSamples`, `ClickGrid` and `Metronome` were compared line by line with `metronome.js` and `click-math.js`.
  - `_parseBytes`, `_dispatch` and `bendValue` were compared with `midi.js`.
  - `applyVelocitySens`, the refcount, pedal OR + invert, CC120/123 and `releaseSource` were compared with
    `controller.js`.
  - No divergence on the paths the fixtures and vectors cover. F1 and F2 are outside them.
- The level param uses `wa::rampTo` (τ 10 ms, pinned at 12 τ), which matches `configure()` in the contract (§1.5,
  §3.10). Click playback is `wa::BufferSource` from T11a, so the Chromium port is T11a's, and there are no
  `fdlibm::` sites in T07's files.

## Fixed in this review (T07's files)

| id | severity | defect | fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | medium | **MIDI clock sent twice after a sample-rate change.** `onAudioEvent(RateChanged)` re-attached the click unit and then called `resetHorizon(now)`. The next pump re-sent every clock pulse of `[now, old horizon)`, which had already gone to the host. A probe at 120 bpm with a rate event at 0.5 s gave 95 pulses instead of 83, and the clock stream ran backwards once. A clock receiver would jump in tempo. | `Metronome::rescheduleTicks(now)` replaces `resetHorizon`. It schedules the ticks of `[now, horizon)` again on the new unit, honouring `quietAfter`, and rebuilds `live`. The horizon and the clock stay as they were. After the fix: 83 pulses, monotonic, and the click at 0.56 s still sounds. Files: `src/click/metronome.{h,cpp}`, `src/click/click_service.cpp`. |
| F2 | low–medium | **Hot-plug did not forget the parser state of the vanished port.** midi.js calls `_detach` → `_parse.delete(id)`. The native parser is keyed by `rig_midi_in`'s uint16, which is T08's `MidiPortTable` index, but the reset used T07's own table index. The two orders differ whenever `rig_midi_in` interned an id before the first ports report. In a probe, a half message `90 3D` sent before a disconnect was completed by the first data byte after the reconnect: it was forwarded, and on the direct path it would play a note that midi.js drops. | Reset every wire index whose `portIdOf()` is the vanished id (T08's mapping, ≤ 128 ids), plus the two previous keys. File: `src/midi/midi_service.cpp`. |

Both fixes were rebuilt incrementally and re-tested on g++, clang and ASan. The fixtures are still bit-exact. The
probes are scratch programs linked against `librig_core.a` (`rate_probe.cpp`, `hotplug_probe.cpp`), because the
review owns only `src/click/**` and `src/midi/**`. T07 should turn them into cases in `tests/test_click.cpp` and
`tests/test_midi.cpp`. The current rate-change case only checks `onsets >= 3`, which would also pass with doubled
clicks or a doubled clock.

| O1 | **high** | orchestrator (`src/graph/graph.cpp`) | **The 'main' click is summed into `outBus_` before the meter and the recorder.** As a result, the recorder always records the click, whatever `record` is set to, and the meter's `out` analysis and `rig_meters` peaks include it. The JS sends 'main' to `ctx.destination` only: `recordTap` hears the click only with `setClick({record:true})` (contract §1.5, §3.11), and `analyserL/R` sit on `fx.out`. A worship-set recording would carry the click. This is the builder's truncated "diff 2". Change: in step 6, only run the unit (`runUnit(UnitSlot::Click, …); clickOn = true;`) and drop `if (!clickAux_) outBus_.add(clickBus_);`. In step 7, feed the meter `outBus_` without the click. Give the recorder `fxBus_ = outBus_; fxBus_.add(clickBus_)` when `clickOn && clickRecord_`, for both main and aux; today this happens only for aux. After the sinks, add `if (clickOn && !clickAux_) outBus_.add(clickBus_);`. Compute `pk` from the pre-click bus by saving the per-channel peak before that add. The goldens are unaffected: the `out` tap is the destination, which keeps the click. Test: render with record off, assert the recorder's input is click-free, then turn record on and assert it is not. |
| O2 | medium | orchestrator (`src/model/patch.h`, `model.cpp`), then T07 | **`setTimeSig(sig, {newBar})` drops `newBar`.** `Model::setTimeSig` ignores its bool, and `SongState` has no field for it. On every song switch, controller.js:318 calls `setTimeSig(…, {newBar: true})`, and the JS click starts a new bar (accent) on the next free beat, even when the tempo and signature are unchanged. Natively, the accent stays on the old bar grid. The smallest change, marked `// INTERFACE-CHANGE(T07)`: add `bool newBar = false;` to `SongState`, and in `Model::setTimeSig` set `song_.newBar = newBar;` before `forEachService(...)` and back to `false` after it. T07 then changes `ClickServiceImpl::onSongState` so the signature branch runs when the signature changed *or* `s.newBar` is set, and passes `s.newBar` to `met_.retime(0, false, sigOf(s), s.newBar)`. Also add a dump-click case "song switch, same sig, newBar" replayed through the service, not only the fake Host. |
| O3 | low | orchestrator (`tests/test_golden.cpp:112-117`) | The builder's diff 1 is correct for now: `rig_render` reports `drone-synth-2keys` as `UNIMPLEMENTED: drone, droneKey` and renders it silent (peak 0). | O5 | low | T08 (`src/api/engine.cpp`) | `midiQ_` is drained only in `controlLoop()` (realtime mode). With device buffers larger than 128 frames, the stamp is often already in the past by the time the control thread drains the queue, so the note plays "now" and the jitter returns.
