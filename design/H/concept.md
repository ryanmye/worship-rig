# H: hybrid. A's one-sound-at-a-time Edit + B's Sunday Keys switches, with a lock that doesn't kill them

**In one line:** Perform keeps today's layout. Each sound gets a lit **ON tile** on top (it's the mute) and **four
modifier chips** at the foot: **Space, Echo, Octave, Sustain**. The shared **Space/Echo presets become one-tap chips**
in the header, and a **Quick sheet** drops over the header only. **Edit** shows **one sound at a time** under a small
live rig map. The panel's title is a **sentence that changes as you edit** ("KEYS plays Grand Piano on every key, a
little into the Hall…"). Its left column is **a copy of that sound's Perform strip**, with the same tile, fader and
chips. **No dials anywhere:** "character" controls are wide sliders labelled in words.

Built from A-v2 (Edit frame, Esc rules, Quick sheet, Sing it in…) and B-v2 (ON tiles, one-tap FX chips, "Song's own",
lock rule, LIVE badge, collapsed empty slot, bright PANIC). The mockups are static HTML generated by `src/build.mjs`.
Rebuild them with `node design/H/src/build.mjs --shots` from the repo root.

## 1. The user's three complaints

| User said | Why it happens (research D1–D7) | What H does |
|---|---|---|
| "Edit is too noisy" | About 17 look-alike cards, plus a right-hand FX column, all peers | Three regions: the library, a **rig map**, and **one focus panel**. Only one sound (or one shared effect) is open at a time. There are at most two levels: the panel, then Advanced. |
| "Everything is just buttons/dials" | One horizontal slider shape does every job, and values are shown in dB and ms | **Each job has one shape, and it's the same shape in both views:** vertical fader = volume · ON tile = on/off · **chip** = a Perform modifier (tap → big steps) · **wide word slider** = the sound's character ("Bright · 100%", with "darker ↔ brighter" at the ends) · preset tiles/chips = pick one. **There are no round knobs anywhere.** A's smart knobs became sliders because the user named dials as the problem. |
| "I feel disconnected from what I'm changing" | Edit doesn't look like Perform, "Reverb" means two things, and nothing shows what's sounding | (1) **The panel's left column is that sound's Perform strip.** It uses the same ON tile, fader and Space/Echo/Octave/Sustain chips, captioned "Same switches as your Perform strip". (2) **The panel title is a sentence** whose underlined words are the settings, and it rewrites itself as you edit. (3) A **coloured line** runs from the selected map block into the panel. (4) Map blocks light from the notes you hold. Send dots and lanes run into Space/Echo/Chorus, matching SPEC §3.1. (5) The keyboard shows each sound's range as a coloured bar. |

And the fourth request: **"settings I can directly change from Perform, like Sunday Keys' toggles".** Perform gets
four modifier chips per sound, the ON tiles, one-tap Space/Echo chips, and a Quick sheet for tempo, swell, touch, pedal
and restart. They all **stay live when Perform is locked** (§4), because a toggle you can't touch during the service
isn't worth having.

## 2. Perform (perform.png)

The positions are today's: wheel · strips · Key & drone · notes · keyboard · Revert / Fade out / PANIC / Lock.

```
top bar   brand · Perform|Edit · [Quick] · ⚙ · MIDI / Sound / READY · REC · meter · Master
header    Song name, KEY [D ▾] you play D · 72 BPM | Transpose − 0 + |
          SPACE  Dry Room Stage [Hall] Cathedral ⋯       (⋯ = Ambient Wash + Vibe)
          ECHO   Off Slap ⌜¼⌟ Dotted⅛ Trails [Song's own 420 ms]              | Chord
setlist   ◀ Prev | chips … | NEXT Building Swell · G ›
stage     Wheel | KEYS | PAD | ┆EXTRA┆ (empty → 60 px dashed column) | BASS | Key & drone | Notes
  strip:  [● KEYS            ON]   ← the ON tile IS mute (tap); OFF = grey tile + amber OFF
          🎹 Grand Piano
          ↻ wheel 100% · (Chorus 35%)   ← read-only line: wheel tag + Chorus badge when non-zero
          vertical fader · −1.9 dB
          [Space 25%][Echo 10%]        ← tap → in-strip steps 100/75/50/25/Off (+ fine slider)
          [Octave −1][● Sustain ]      ← Octave → steps +2…−2 · Sustain = direct toggle
  drone:  [● DRONE D major   ON] [Synth|My Pads] · 12-key grid · Major/Minor + Level · Brightness · Movement · toggles
bottom    keyboard with range bars (display only) | ↺ Revert | Fade out | PANIC (Esc) | Lock
```

**What each piece is:**
- **ON tile.** It replaces the small MUTE button at the bottom of each strip (it is `slots.i.muted`, which already
  remembers the gain from before the mute). ON is a slot-coloured tile with a glowing LED. OFF is a grey tile with a
  **solid amber OFF**, the loudest thing in the strip, and the rest of the strip greys out (perform-off.png): name dimmed,
  fader fill grey, dB struck through, chips as grey outlines, and a grey range bar over the keyboard. Moving MUTE to the
  top also removes the old "Chorus chip is 8 px above MUTE" hazard, because nothing sits under the chips any more.
- **Modifier chips** are 46 px tall (42 at 1024) with 13 px bold labels. **Lit = solid slot-colour fill with dark text**;
  that means Space/Echo > 0 or Octave ≠ 0. Unlit = grey outline. Sustain is an LED toggle: lit LED when on (the default),
  and an **amber "Sus. off"** when off, because that's the "why is my bass choppy?" state. The amount bar along a chip's
  bottom edge shows the level.
  - **Space / Echo / Octave:** tap to open an **in-strip step panel** that sits entirely inside its own strip and covers
    only that strip's fader (perform-step.png, perform-space-1024.png). **Tap a step and it's done:** the panel closes.
    Space/Echo also have a thin fine slider. A click outside closes the panel **and is swallowed**, so it can't grab a
    neighbouring fader.
  - **Sustain:** tap flips `slots.i.sustain`. It's a real boolean, so there's no hidden state.
- **Chorus** is a read-only badge on the tag line when non-zero. You change it in Edit, where the mini strip has a
  fifth, wide Chorus chip. It isn't in Perform today, so nothing is lost.
- **Space/Echo chips** in the header apply the existing presets in one tap. The selected chip is a **mid-grey fill with
  a white 2 px border**: clearly selected, but never brighter than PANIC, and never a slot colour (shared = neutral).
  A custom value shows as **"Song's own 420 ms"** (selected), with a **dashed outline on the nearest preset** (¼).
  Once you've picked another echo, tapping "Song's own" restores just that effect from the snapshot Perform already
  keeps for Revert. That is the undo for "I tapped Off and lost the song's custom echo", without reverting the faders
  too. The chip only appears when the song was loaded with a custom value. At 1024 the rows collapse into two pills
  (Space: Hall ▾ / Echo: Song's own ▾) that open the same chips (perform-1024.png).
- **KEY ▾ → Sing it in…** (perform-key.png) writes `hearIn` via the existing transpose path, while the player's hands
  stay in D. The header then reads "G · you play D", and Transpose shows +5 in accent. "Back to D" undoes it.
- **Next** is a button at the right end of the setlist row, opposite Prev.
- **Empty EXTRA** collapses to a 60 px dashed column ("+ EXTRA · empty"), which gives the three real strips about
  170 px each at 1440 (today they're about 130 px). With four sounds loaded, the grid returns to four equal strips.
- **PANIC** is a solid red fill with white 24 px text and a soft glow. It is the brightest control on the screen.
- **Fader throw:** about 250 px at 1440 (today's is about 315 px, B-v2's 258 px) and about 140 px at 1024. That's the
  price of the chip block, and it's the main number to watch (see §7).

### Quick sheet (quick.png, quick-1024.png)
It drops from **Quick** in the top bar over the **header and setlist rows only**, at every size. Strips, drone,
keyboard, Revert, Fade out, PANIC and Lock all stay visible and live. It's non-modal, so the keys keep playing. It
closes on ✕, Q, **Esc (which also panics)**, or **any song change**.

| Section | Contents (all existing) | Under lock |
|---|---|---|
| **This song** · saved with the song · Revert undoes | Tempo (BPM + big **TAP**; hint: "Echo is synced (¼), so it follows"), Swell time stepper | live |
| **This Mac** · every song · soundcheck settings | Touch (Light/Normal/Heavy/Fixed), Sustain pedal: **live PEDAL DOWN light** + Reversed | frozen (the light stays live) |
| **If something's wrong** | Sound status; **hold 1 s to restart** while OK, a primary **Restart audio** when stalled | live |
| header | "When locked: This song stays live, This Mac is frozen" · All settings · ✕ | — |

**Mono output** and **Keyboard picks songs** stay in Settings only. They're soundcheck settings, and flipping them
mid-set changes the PA feed or makes patch buttons jump songs.

## 3. Edit (edit.png, edit-pad.png, edit-space.png, edit-1024.png)

```
┌ library ┐┌ [● LIVE] Sunday Pad + Piano · Key D · you play D ▾ · 72 BPM Tap · Notes · "The song that's playing…" · ⋯ Song ┐
│ setlist ││ YOUR RIG: Keyboard → KEYS PAD +EXTRA BASS DRONE ══ dry ══▶ Tape → Master   (blocks light from held notes)       │
│ songs   ││           → Space / → Echo / → Chorus lanes, dots sized by each send ──▶ Space Hall · Echo Custom · Chorus       │
│         ││ ───────── coloured line from the selected block ─────────                                                       │
│         ││ [icon] KEYS plays Grand Piano on every key, a little into the Hall, sustain on        [Change instrument ▾]    │
│         ││ ON STAGE (= Perform strip)  │ THE SOUND ITSELF (word sliders)      │ WHERE IT PLAYS                               │
│         ││ [● KEYS ON]                 │ Brightness   ━━━━━━━━━━━●  Bright 100% │ Keyboard range · every key [mini keys ▭▭] │
│         ││ fader │ [Space][Echo]       │ Warmth       ━━━━━●━━━━━  Neutral 0 dB │ [Set lowest…] [Set highest…]               │
│         ││ −1.9  │ [Oct  ][Sus ]       │ Ring-out     ━━━●━━━━━━━  Natural 0.35 s│ Response · this sound only [Soft|Normal…] │
│         ││       │ [ Chorus off ]      │                                         │                                            │
│         ││ › Advanced  Pan Center · Width 100% · Highs 0 dB · Transpose 0 · Voices All · Response Normal · Pitch bend Off   │
└─────────┘└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
 keyboard row: legend · 61 keys with range bars (display only) · output meter · Fade out · PANIC ⌘.
```

- **The sentence title** comes from B. It names only what differs from normal, and the underlined words jump to their
  control. It is presentation only: a pure function of the slot (`describeSlot()`).
- **The ON STAGE column** reuses Perform's `onTile` and `modChip` components, bound to the same paths. Tapping a chip
  opens the same step panel. So a volunteer who learned the Perform strip already knows this column. Chorus is the
  fifth chip here (Edit only).
- **The sound itself** has three **word sliders**, and each is a view of exactly one existing param, chosen by a rule
  (no new state):
  - Brightness = `params.cutoff` → `params.tone` → `slots.i.eq.high`
  - Warmth = `slots.i.eq.low` (bipolar, filled from the centre)
  - Fade-in = `params.attack` for pad-like instruments, otherwise Ring-out = `params.release`

  Every other instrument param sits under Advanced, as "<Instrument> engine".
- **Where it plays:** the key-range picker is its own mini keyboard with handles, plus "Set lowest… / Set highest…"
  (tap, then play the key). The bars over the playable keyboard are **display only**, so a drag can't play a note into
  the PA. The per-sound velocity curve is labelled **Response** so it doesn't clash with the global **Touch** in Quick.
- **Shared-effect panels** (edit-space.png): the sentence title ("SPACE is a Hall. Keys and Pad go into it; Bass stays
  dry."), preset tiles with the existing blurbs, **Fine-tune as word sliders**, and **"Who's sending in"** as a
  slot-coloured row of the **same Off/25/50/75/100 steps** as each sound's Space chip. A custom send gets a dashed
  outline on the nearest step. Vibe sits in the footer.
- **LIVE badge** plus "The song that's playing. Changes are heard now and saved with it." This is today's behaviour,
  now said out loud.
- **Destructive actions moved away from live controls** (from A-v2): Reset to factory lives in **⋯ Song**, and Clear
  slot is **Change instrument ▾ → Remove this sound…**. Both ask first, and Remove offers Undo.
- **Fade out and PANIC** appear in Edit's keyboard row. PANIC shows **⌘ .**, because in Edit Esc still cancels text
  entry, as it does today.
- **At 1024** the library becomes "☰ Songs", helper text hides, and nothing scrolls (edit-1024.png).

## 4. The lock rule: lock protects the song file and the rig, not your playing

The only real behaviour change in H. Today, lock freezes transpose, the key grid, the drone mode and the FX pickers,
which would kill every Sunday Keys–style switch in the mode people actually play in (B critique #4).

| While locked | Controls | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Live** (one tap, as unlocked) | faders · all 4 **ON tiles** · **drone ON tile** + Level · wheel/Swell · **strip chips** (Space, Echo, Octave, Sustain) · **header Space/Echo chips** incl. Song's own · Quick › This song (Tempo/TAP, Swell time) · Prev/Next · Fade out · PANIC · pedal light · Restart | These are playing moves, and Revert or Song's own undoes all of them |
| **Hold 600 ms** (amber HOLD tag, the same gesture as unlock) | Transpose −/+ · KEY ▾ Sing it in… · drone key grid · Major/Minor · Revert | A wrong key is audible in every note, so make it deliberate. Still no unlock → change → re-lock routine |
| **Frozen** (dimmed) | Edit tab · ⚙ Settings · drone Synth/My Pads, Brightness, Movement, toggles, pad folder · Quick › This Mac (Touch, Reversed) · setlist reorder | Soundcheck and file-level settings |

`perform-locked.png` shows this, and the lock button's tooltip copy changes to match. If the owner wants today's
stricter lock back, the "Live" row can simply revert to frozen; the layout doesn't depend on it.

## 7. Honest limits and open questions
- **Fader throw drops about 20 % at 1440** (from about 315 px to about 250 px) and to about 140 px at 1024. The chip
  block costs about 100 px. If players feel it, drop the Octave/Sustain row on the surface and show them as badges (A's
  critic's version). The layout works either way. *[infer, untested with players]*
- **The looser lock is a behaviour change.** Anyone who relied on lock freezing Space/Echo loses that. ui-core tests
  asserting "X disabled when locked" need updating.
- **Hold-to-change is a hidden gesture.** The amber HOLD tags appear only while locked, and a short press should flash
  "hold to change key" (the existing lock-hint pattern).
- **Two 12-key grids with different meanings** (Sing it in… vs Key & drone) still need a test with a real volunteer
  (carried over from A-v2).
- **Sing it in… covers the top of the Keys/Pad strips (and the Keys fader handle) at 1440** while it's open. It closes
  on the first key tap, and outside clicks are swallowed, so a stray click can't move a fader.
- **Map lights come from held notes, not audio.** Reverb tails don't light anything.
- **The sentence copy and word buckets** ("Mellow", "a little into the Hall") are my wording and haven't been tested
  with users. The number is always shown beside the word.
- **The research on Sunday Keys' exact layout** came from support-doc text, not screenshots (research.md says so).
  The "toggles" here follow what those docs describe (named modifiers per section, engage-on-main-screen, parameters one
  tap deeper). They don't reproduce a verified screen.
