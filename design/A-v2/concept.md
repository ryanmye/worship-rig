# Angle A v2: "Instrument-first", revised after the live-usability critique

**One line:** In Edit you work on **one sound at a time**, in a panel attached by a coloured line to a small **live rig
map**. Perform keeps its layout and muscle memory. It gains **two big Space/Echo chips per strip**, a proper
**muted** look, a **"Sing it in…"** key picker, and a **Quick settings sheet** that drops down over the song header. The
sheet never covers a strip, the drone, the keyboard or PANIC.

This version keeps v1's Edit architecture and reworks the parts that would fail on a Sunday morning.

Screens (1440×900 unless noted; the HTML sources sit next to them, generated by `src/build.mjs --shots`):

## 1. The user's three complaints, and how A answers them (unchanged from v1, still the core)

| User's words | Cause | What A does |
|---|---|---|
| "Too noisy" | About 17 identical cards compete, including a right-hand FX column | Edit has 3 regions: library, **rig map**, and **one focus panel**. Only one thing is open at a time, with at most 2 disclosure levels (panel, then Advanced). |
| "Everything is buttons/dials" | One slider shape used for every job, with values in dB and ms | **One shape per job:** vertical fader = volume; round knob = this sound's character (Brightness / Warmth / Fade-in or Ring-out); "pipe" slider with an arrow = how much goes into a shared effect; chips = pick one. Values are shown as words first ("Bright · 100%"). |
| "Disconnected from what I'm changing" | Edit doesn't look like Perform; "Reverb" means two things; nothing shows what is sounding | The map blocks use Perform's strip order and colours. **A line in the block's colour runs into the panel.** The send pipes and the map dots are the same value. Blocks light from held notes, and the keyboard shows each sound's range as a coloured bar. |

The smart-knob mapping is still **one existing param per knob, chosen by a rule, with no new state**:
- Brightness = `params.cutoff` → `params.tone` → `slots.i.eq.high`
- Warmth = `slots.i.eq.low` (bipolar)
- Fade-in = `params.attack` (pad-like instruments), otherwise Ring-out = `params.release`

The rule lives in `shared/smart-controls.js` (see implementation.md).

## 2. Response to the critique, item by item

The critique arrived cut off in the middle of its 1024-drawer item. Everything I received is answered below. For the
truncated item I assumed "don't cover the drone at 1024".

| # | Critique | Verdict | What v2 does | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Esc hazard.** v1's popover "closes on Esc without triggering panic" | **Accepted, and it's the most important fix** | In Perform, **Esc always panics**, and it also closes any chip panel, the key picker or the Quick sheet in the same keypress. These overlays are deliberately **not** `role="dialog"`, because today's `popupOpen()` in main.js swallows Esc whenever a dialog is visible. They are also exempt from the "first Esc just blurs the focused control" rule. PANIC shows `Esc` on its face. Required test: overlay open → Esc → `controller.panic()` called **and** overlay closed. | perform.png (PANIC · Esc) |
| 2 | **Dangerous toggles in the drawer** (Keyboard picks songs, Mono output, Restart audio) | **Accepted** | *Keyboard picks songs* and *Mono output* go back to All settings only, because they're setup-time. **Restart audio** is a quiet **"Hold to restart audio"** link (1 s press-and-hold with a filling ring) while sound is OK. It becomes a normal one-click **primary** button only when status ≠ OK. | quick.png vs quick-1024.png |
| 3 | Chips too small and faint for low light; the 2×2 block sits 8 px above MUTE | **Accepted** | **2 chips per strip (Space, Echo)**, 46 px tall (44 at 1024), with a 13 px bold label. **Lit = solid slot-colour fill with dark text.** Off = grey outline with grey text. There's a 10 px gap to MUTE, plus a 7 px flex gap, so ≥16 px in total. | perform.png |
| 3b | Chorus/Octave as a badge line only when non-default | **Accepted** | Read-only badges under the instrument name ("Chorus 35%", "Octave −1"). The line is always reserved, so faders stay aligned across strips. You change them in Edit. They were never on today's Perform, so nothing is lost. | perform.png (Pad, Bass) |
| 4 | Popover knob is the wrong gesture, wrong place (it spreads over the Keys fader); Octave shouldn't be a knob | **Accepted, with a modification** | No knob. Tapping a chip opens an **in-strip step panel**: **100 · 75 · 50 · 25 · Off** as big stacked buttons, plus a thin fine slider. **Tap a step and it's done:** the panel closes. It sits **entirely inside its own strip** and never touches another strip. *Modification:* I stacked the steps vertically instead of in the suggested horizontal row. A strip is about 136 px wide at 1440 (118 at 1024), so 5 horizontal steps would be about 24 px each, below the touch-size floor the critique asks for. The vertical stack also matches the fader direction. Octave is no longer on the Perform surface (see 3b); in Edit it is already −2…+2 segmented. Click-outside closes the panel **and the click is swallowed**, so it can't grab a fader. | perform-chip.png, perform-chip-1024.png |
| 5 | Muted strip not designed | **Accepted** | Muted = strip header, badges and top border dim; the fader fill turns grey; the dB is struck through; the chips drop to grey outlines (their values stay readable); the range bar above the keyboard goes grey. **MUTE becomes a solid amber "MUTED" button**, the loudest thing in the strip. | perform-muted.png |
| 6 | No quick way to change key when the leader says "in G" | **Accepted** | The **KEY badge is now a button** (D ▾). It opens **"Sing it in…"**, a 12-key grid. Tapping G makes the band hear G while **your hands stay in D**. That's the existing Easy Transpose `hearIn` via the controller's transpose path; `transposeOctave` is untouched. The header then reads "KEY G · you play D" and Transpose reads **+5** in accent. "Back to D" undoes it. The ± stepper stays for +1 changes. The picker is frozen under Perform-lock. It also points to the existing **Key & drone** grid for "I'll play in G too". That grid writes `hearIn` and `playIn` together (today's `setSongKey`). v1 had mislabelled that block "Key drone"; it is the song key, so v2 labels it **Key & drone**, as the app does today. | perform-key.png |
| 7 | The drawer duplicates the Space/Echo pills and misses Tap tempo | **Accepted** | The drawer's "This song" section is now **Tempo (BPM + big TAP)** and **Swell time** (a stepper over `song.patch.swell.seconds`, 1–60 s). Both are existing song fields that only Edit could reach. **Revert needs no change:** `SAVED_FIELDS` in perform.js already includes `tempo` and `patch` (swell lives in `patch`). The critic's worry came from a stale comment beside it ("tempo only changes in Edit"). Fix that comment. | quick.png |
| 8 | At 1024 the drawer covers the whole drone block and the Bass chips, and nothing prompts you to close it | **Accepted, redesigned** | The side drawer is now a **top sheet**. It drops from the Quick button over **only the song header and setlist rows**, at every size. Strips, Key & drone, keyboard, Revert, Fade out, PANIC and Lock all stay visible and live. It closes on ✕, Q, **Esc (which also panics)**, and **automatically on any song change** (Next, Prev, MIDI program change). You can't carry it into the next song by accident. Clicks outside pass through normally, because it covers nothing you'd reach for mid-song. That is the one place I don't swallow outside clicks (see §3). | quick.png, quick-1024.png |
| 9 | Destructive buttons next to live controls in Edit (Reset to factory, Clear slot) | **Accepted** | **Reset to factory** moves into a **"⋯ Song"** menu (alongside Duplicate and Rename) and asks first. **Clear slot** becomes **"Remove this sound…"** at the bottom of the Change instrument menu, below a divider and in red. | 9b | Edit has no PANIC or Fade out on screen | **Accepted** | **Fade out** and **PANIC** sit at the right of Edit's keyboard row, the same place as in Perform. PANIC shows **⌘ .**, because in Edit Esc still cancels text entry and menus. That is today's rule, and it has to stay: you type song names and notes in Edit. | edit.png, edit-1024.png |
| 10 | Draggable range-bar ends sit on top of the playable keyboard | **Accepted** | The bars above Edit's bottom keyboard are **display only**, with a 9 px gap above the keys. A drag can no longer land on a playable key. | edit.png |

**Rejected or modified, summarised:**
- (4) Steps are stacked vertically rather than in a horizontal row, because of the strip width. The one-tap intent is kept.
- (8) The Quick sheet lets outside clicks through, because it covers nothing live.
- (7) The Revert list needs no change: tempo and patch are already in it. Only the comment is fixed.

Nothing else from the critique was rejected.

## 3. Overlay rules in Perform (one table, so implementation can't drift)

| Overlay | Opens from | Covers | Closes on | Click outside | Esc | Under Perform-lock |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Space/Echo step panel | chip | its **own strip only** (above the chips) | step tap, ✕, other chip, song change | closes, **click swallowed** | **panic** + close | chips are display-only; the panel won't open |
| Sing it in… | KEY ▾ | song header, setlist row, top of the Keys/Pad strips (clear of the fader handles at 1440) | key tap (applies **and** closes: one tap), ✕, song change | closes, **click swallowed** | **panic** + close | won't open (the key is frozen, as today) |
| Space / Echo pill menus | header pills | header area | pick, ✕, song change | closes, swallowed | **panic** + close | frozen (as today) |
| Quick sheet | Quick / Q | song header + setlist rows **only** | ✕, Q, song change | passes through | **panic** + close | opens **read-only** (pedal light still live) |

None of these use `role="dialog"`, so main.js's `popupOpen()` never swallows Esc for them. Settings stays a real modal:
Esc closes it and doesn't panic, as today.

## 4. Information architecture

### Perform (perform.png)
The layout is unchanged from today: wheel strip, 4 strips, Key & drone, notes, keyboard, Revert / Fade out / PANIC /
Lock. Additions:
1. **Strip:** a group icon above the name (hidden at 1024 to give the fader room), a reserved read-only **badge line**,
   then fader, dB, **Space and Echo chips**, and MUTE.
2. **KEY ▾** button → Sing it in….
3. The Space/Vibe selects become **Space and Echo pills**. Each shows the preset or "Custom ≈ Quarter" (the nearest
   preset, presentation only). Vibe chips sit inside the Space pill menu.
4. **Key & drone:** the mode, key grid, major/minor and level as today, plus **Brightness / Movement as small horizontal
   sliders** with word values. v1 used knobs here; that's the same trackpad-gesture objection as critique #4.
5. **Range bars** above the keyboard, in slot colours; a muted slot's bar is grey.
6. **Quick** button (sliders icon) beside the gear.

### Quick sheet (quick.png)
| Section | Contents (all existing) | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| **This song** | Tempo (BPM readout + TAP; the hint says the echo follows it when `fx.delay.sync` ≠ off), Swell time | saved with the song; Revert restores both |
| **Every song** | Touch (`settings.velocitySens`), Sustain pedal: a live light + Reversed (`settings.pedalInvert`) | this Mac |
| **If something's wrong** | Sound status; hold-to-restart (OK) or a primary Restart (not OK) → `controller.restartAudio()` | — |
| header | "Read-only while Perform is locked" · All settings · ✕ | — |

## 6. Honest limits and open questions
- **The "lights" come from held notes, not audio.** Reverb tails and pedal-sustained decay don't light anything.
  Per-slot meters would need engine analysers, which is out of scope.
- **The in-strip step panel hides that strip's own fader while it's open.** It closes on the first step tap, so this
  lasts about 1 s in normal use. If players want to ride a fader and set Space at the same moment, this is the wrong
  trade. The alternative is a panel anchored *below* the strip over the keyboard card, which I rejected because that
  covers the on-screen keys.
- **Two 12-key grids (Sing it in…, Key & drone) with different meanings** is a real risk for a volunteer. The
  copy in the picker ("Your hands stay in D") and the header readout ("KEY G · you play D", Transpose +5 in accent) are
  meant to carry it, but this needs a test with a real player. The fallback is to drop Sing it in… and keep only the
  ± stepper, with a live "band hears G" readout.
- **Word buckets** ("Bright", "Slow") are my own copy and haven't been user-tested. The number is always shown beside
  the word.
- **Hold-to-restart** is a new interaction; it's the only press-and-hold control on the screen besides Lock's
  hold-to-unlock, so the pattern already exists in the app.
- The map mirrors the SPEC §3.1 topology. If the engine topology changes, the map changes with it.
