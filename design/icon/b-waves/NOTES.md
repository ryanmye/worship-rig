# B · Swell

**Idea.** One sustained note drawn as its envelope (soft attack, long flat hold, soft release), echoing down as three
stacked ribbons: a pad you can feel. Brass on top is the one warm accent; two plum
echoes fade below, under a faint brass glow. Mono = the same three swells as black strokes.

**Palette.** Ground #241a38 → #100a1c (Sanctuary plum); brass #f4d48a → #f4b73f (Sanctuary #e6ba65 lifted
toward Daylight Day #f4b73f); echoes #a47ab8, #644779.

**16 px check.** v1 (filled, nested mounds with dark gaps) read as a bed/tunnel, and the brass leaked at the edges. v2
(ribbons) overflowed the squircle. v3 pulls the group inside the body, thins the ribbons toward the bottom (96/84/76),
and opens the pitch to 172 with a flatter rise. Now three distinct stripes at 16 px. The tray at 22 px
reads as three clean chevron-swells.
