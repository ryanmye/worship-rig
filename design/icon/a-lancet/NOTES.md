# A · Lancet

**Idea.** The Sanctuary v2 mark grown into a Dock icon. The window group is mark.svg's own 32-unit geometry at ×22,
centred in the squircle. The glass gives off light: pane bloom, a candle-oculus halo and a warm wall glow. A brass sill
grounds the window.

**Palette.** Plum `#3a2558→#231539→#180e2b`. Brass `#f7de9c/#e6ba65/#a9772d`, sill to `#9a6b1c`.
Panes: orange `#ffa878→#ee7843`, green `#72dfae→#3aaa7b`, blue `#a6e3fc→#58b9e6`, violet `#c7a2f2→#9163cc`.
Oculus `#fffbef→#e9cf92`. Leading `#0c0716`.

**16 px.** The brass frame blurred into the plum, so I widened it from 2.0 to 2.3 units and scaled the window from
×21 to ×22. The sheen was lighting the tympanum, so it now covers the panes only. I lifted the body bottom for
dark-Dock contrast. In the tray icon, the dot over solid panes read as ♀. It is now drawn as strokes.
