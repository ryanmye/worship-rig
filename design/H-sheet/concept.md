# H-sheet: H's Perform, with Edit as a song sheet

**In one line:** Perform, Quick and the lock rule are H's, unchanged. Edit becomes a **song sheet**: one plain-English
line per sound ("KEYS plays Grand Piano on every key, a little into the Hall, a touch of echo, sustain on"). The
**underlined words are the controls**, and the ones that match a Perform chip open **the same steps as that chip**.
Tapping a line opens a compact panel with **3–4 word sliders** (no knobs), and each open line has an **Advanced**
fold for the rest. Nothing is more than two levels deep.

The mockups are static HTML generated by `src/build.mjs`. Rebuild them with `node design/H-sheet/src/build.mjs --shots`
from the repo root. `perform.mjs`, `quick.mjs`, `base.css` and `helpers.mjs` are **byte-for-byte copies of `design/H/src`**.
The brief says "H-v2", but no `design/H-v2/` existed when this was built, so Perform here is H's Perform. If H-v2 lands,
copy its `perform.mjs` over this one; the Edit page doesn't depend on it.

## 1. Why this answers the user

| User said | H (map + panel) | H-sheet |
|---|---|---|
| "Edit is too noisy (everything is just buttons/dials)" | A rig map, 3 panel columns, and a mini strip of 5 chips. It's calmer than today, but it's still a control surface. | **The page is text.** Collapsed lines show a sentence, an ON tile and a level slider, and nothing else. The only real controls are in the **one open line**: three word sliders and a range picker. There are **no knobs, and no chip grid, anywhere in Edit.** |
| "I feel disconnected from what I'm changing" | The sentence was the panel title, and the map lit up | **The sentence is the whole interface.** The thing you read ("a little into the Hall") is the thing you tap, and after the change it reads differently ("half into the Hall"). The ON tile is the Perform tile. The step popover is the Perform step panel, and it names the chip it matches ("Same steps as the **Space 25%** chip on your Perform strip"). Opening a line dims the other range bars on the keyboard row. |
| "Settings I can directly change from Perform (Sunday Keys' toggles)" | ON tiles, 4 chips per strip, header Space/Echo chips, Quick sheet, looser lock | **Unchanged from H.** Edit reinforces it: each Perform chip is a *phrase* in the sheet, so a volunteer who edits a song learns the chips by name. |
| "Shouldn't change functionality much" | No new store fields | Same. **No new store fields, PARAMS rows or engine code.** The sentence is a pure function of the slot. |

## 2. Anatomy of the sheet (edit.png)

```
top bar     (H's, unchanged)
library     setlist ▾ · "Tap a song to switch to it. It starts playing." · songs · New / Factory / Library · Export/import
song head   [● LIVE] Sunday Pad + Piano · KEY D · you play D ▾ · 72 BPM Tap · Notes · "The song that's playing…" · ⋯ Song
sheet       One line per sound. Tap an underlined word to change it: they're the same switches as your Perform strip.
  [● KEYS  ON]  plays Grand Piano on every key, a little into the Hall, a touch of echo, sustain on  ━━●━ −1.9 dB  [⌄]
     SHAPE THE PIANO   Brightness ━━━━━● Bright | Warmth ━━●━━ Neutral | Ring-out ━●━━━ Natural   │ WHERE IT PLAYS [▭▭▭▭▭]
                                                                                                     │ [Set lowest…][Set highest…]
     › Advanced  Octave normal · Chorus off · Pan Center · Width 100% · Highs 0 dB · …        [all at default]
  [● PAD   ON]  plays Warm Pad, swells with the wheel, half into the Hall, a touch of echo, some chorus  ━━●━ −5.2 dB [›]
  [+ EXTRA   ]  is empty. Choose a sound to layer on top, or split the keyboard.                                  [›]
  [● BASS  ON]  plays Sub Bass below middle C, an octave down, one note at a time, dry, sustain off(amber)  ━━●━  [›]
  [● DRONE ON]  hums a synth D major chord, soft and gently moving, carries on into the next song        ━━●━ +1.6 [›]
  [⌨ HANDS   ]  the mod wheel and pedal bring in the Pad, bend lifts the drone, Swell takes 8 s                   [›]
  [⌂ EFFECTS ]  Space: Hall · Echo: song's own 420 ms · Chorus: gentle · Tape: off · Vibe: your own mix  every sound shares these [›]
keyboard    legend · 61 keys with range bars (display only; the open sound's bar stays bright) · output · Fade out · PANIC ⌘.
```

### A line has four parts, and they're the same on every line
1. **ON tile.** This is the Perform strip's head, restyled smaller (38 px tall). Tapping it toggles `slots.i.muted`.
   It glows while the sound is playing. HANDS and EFFECTS get a plain label tile instead, because they aren't sounds.
   EXTRA gets a dashed "+" tile.
2. **Sentence.** It starts with the role, which is the tile. It **names only what differs from normal** (from B), and
   each underlined word is a button. Words for shared effects ("Hall") are underlined in the neutral effects colour;
   everything else uses the slot colour. A warning state uses the same amber as Perform ("sustain **off**" matches
   Perform's amber "Sus. off" chip).
3. **Level.** A horizontal slider plus dB, bound to the same `slots.i.gain` as the Perform fader.
4. **› / ⌄.** Opens or closes the line. Only one line is open at a time.

### Three ways to change something, with at most two levels
| Gesture | Opens | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Tap a word** (level 1) | A popover anchored under the word: steps, a toggle, or a short list | "a little" → 100/75/50/25/Off (edit-word.png); "sustain on" flips in place to "sustain off"; "one note at a time" → Chords / Lowest note / Highest note |
| **Open the line** (level 1) | 3–4 **word sliders** for the sound's character, plus *Where it plays* | Brightness · Warmth · Ring-out (a pad gets Fade-in instead of Ring-out, using H's one-param rule) |
| **Advanced** (level 2, inside the open line) | Everything else for that line, in a 3-column fold | Mix (Chorus, Pan, Width, Highs) · Playing (Octave, Transpose, Voices, Response, Pitch bend) · *Instrument* engine |

**Rule for defaults:** a Perform-chip setting at its default (Octave normal, Chorus off) has no words in the
sentence. It's listed first in the Advanced summary line and can be changed there (or on the Perform strip in one tap).
**As soon as it isn't default, it gets a phrase** ("an octave down", "some chorus"), which is then a tappable word.
This keeps the sentences short without hiding any control.

### Word ↔ number buckets (shown in the step popover, so they're learnable)
Sends (Space/Echo/Chorus): **Off = dry · 25 = a little / a touch · 50 = half / some · 75 = mostly · 100 = all the way**.
In-between values (from the fine slider) use the nearest word. The popover always shows the number next to the word, and
the open line's word sliders show both ("Bright 100%").

| Perform (H) | In the sheet | Opens |

## 3. The other open lines (not all mocked; same pattern)

| Line | Word sliders when open (3–4) | Words → | Advanced |
|---|---|---|---|
| **KEYS / PAD / EXTRA / BASS** | Brightness, Warmth, Ring-out *or* Fade-in (H's `smartSlidersFor`) + *Where it plays* | instrument → grouped instrument menu (from H: **Remove this sound…** is last, asks, Undo); range → the range picker; sends/octave/sustain/voices → popovers | Chorus, Pan, Width, Highs · Octave, Transpose, Voices, Response, Pitch bend · *Instrument engine* (every other instrument param) |
| **EXTRA (empty)** | — | "Choose a sound" → instrument menu; "split the keyboard" → instrument menu, then the new sound opens with its range picker armed | — |
| **DRONE** | Brightness, Movement, Key fade, Width | "synth" → Synth / My Pads; "D major" → key grid + Major/Minor; "soft"/"gently moving" → sliders; "carries on" → toggle | Chord follow (synth only), Minor uses relative-major file, pad folder (→ Settings) |
| **HANDS** | Wheel range (From–To), Pedal range (From–To), Bend range (semitones), Swell time | "mod wheel"/"pedal" → target list (Keys/Pad/Extra/Bass/Drone level, Reverb level, …); "lifts the drone" → bend mode list | Volume knob (CC7) target |
| **EFFECTS** (edit-fx.png) | Space size, Space level, Echo repeats, Chorus | "Hall" / "song's own 420 ms" → focus the chip rows; "Tape: off" → Advanced › Tape; "Vibe" → Vibe chips | Space darkness, pre-delay · Echo time, tone, ping-pong, sync · Chorus rate, level · Tape (6) · Finish: EQ, glue, master |

The EFFECTS line also has **"Who goes in: Keys a little · Pad half · Bass dry"**, using the same words as the sound
lines. It's the sheet's answer to H's map lanes: it answers "where does sound go?" in words.

**Data note:** the brief's example line was "Space: Hall · Echo: dotted eighth · Vibe: Sunday". The mockup shows
"Echo: song's own 420 ms · Vibe: your own mix" because that's the song state in H's Perform mockup (custom echo, "Song's
own" chip lit). A song saved on presets reads exactly like the brief's example.

| | 1440×900 | 1024×700 |
|---|---|---|
| Sheet height | 628 px | 496 px |
| Open KEYS line | 230 px | 179 px |
| Collapsed line | 48–50 px | 40–43 px |
| Lines fully visible | **all 7** | **all 7** (the brief asked for 3) |
| Word slider width | 243 px | 227 px |
| Underlined word height | ≥ 26.8 px | ≥ 23.1 px (see §6) |

At 1024: the library becomes "☰ Songs", helper text and slider numbers hide (the word stays), the tile and fonts shrink,
and the keyboard row keeps Fade out + PANIC. Opening Advanced or EFFECTS scrolls the sheet, and the open line scrolls
into view (`scrollIntoView({block:'nearest'})`). That's shown in edit-advanced.png and edit-fx.png.

## 6. What H-sheet gives up versus H, and the risks

- **No rig map.** H's map showed signal flow (sound → effect lanes → Tape → Master) and lit up from held notes. The
  sheet says it in words instead ("half into the Hall", "Who goes in"), and the ON-tile LEDs light from held notes. A
  player who thinks in routing may miss the picture. *[infer; untested]*
- **Sentence copy carries the design.** The buckets ("a touch", "some", "gently moving") are my wording, not tested
  with volunteers. The number is always one tap away (popover or slider), but a bad word would mislead. The copy rules
  must live in one pure module with tests (see implementation.md).
- **Word tap targets are 23–27 px tall.** That's fine for an Edit page used with a mouse or trackpad between songs, but
  below the 44 px touch guideline. Mitigation: each word's hit box extends vertically to the full line height (a
  `::before` with negative insets); neighbouring words are separated by plain text, so the boxes don't overlap
  horizontally.
- **Sentences reflow when values change.** "a little" → "half" moves the words after it by a few pixels. The popover
  stays anchored to the word it opened from, and closes on a step tap. Word slider drags don't touch the sentence until
  release (it re-renders on `change`, not `input`).
- **Defaults are hidden from the sentence.** Octave and Chorus for a sound at default are only in Advanced (and on the
  Perform strip). The Advanced summary lists them first, so this is one extra click in Edit and zero clicks in Perform.
- **Long lines wrap at 1024** for unusual songs: five non-default phrases plus a long instrument name. They wrap to 2
  lines; the sheet scrolls. In the mockup song, the longest line (BASS, 6 phrases) fits on one line at both sizes. I
  haven't checked every factory song. *[unverified]*
- **Perform risks are H's** (fader throw about 250 px at 1440, looser lock, hold gesture). See `design/H/concept.md` §7.
