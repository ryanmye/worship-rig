Slot EQ on the piano

Two working prototypes, one recommendation. Nothing under app/ changed. Full reasoning in design/eq/DECISION.md.

Build “curve”: four parametric bands (low shelf, two bells, high shelf) plus a low cut, drawn over a pitch-true keyboard with a Hz ruler and typed Hz / note / dB / Q fields.

Today’s eq.low and eq.high become its two shelves unchanged, so existing songs, MIDI mappings and H’s Brightness slider need no migration. A pasted EQ from another tool maps across line for line, with no fitting.

From “octaves” we keep the paste box, the rule that the zone above your top note stays live (it’s the tone of your notes), and live re-greying when the range changes. The simple mode is H’s Brightness and Warmth sliders, not octave bars.

What ships

PieceDecision
Paramseq.low, eq.high (existing) + eq.lowHz 120, eq.highHz 6000, eq.mid1/mid1Hz/mid1Q, eq.mid2/…, eq.cutHz (20 = off). Frequencies are stored in Hz: a MIDI-note float didn’t round-trip for 30 of 39 common values. Two-segment names are needed because the store only nests one level.
EngineSlot strip wOut → low cut (dry/wet) → low shelf → bell → bell → high shelf → pan. 0 dB filters are bit-exact identity, so there’s no bypass switching. k-rate params cut the ramping cost 3.6×.
Edit (H-v2)Advanced → Tone panel with the keyboard EQ, and the number table behind one disclosure. A mini curve sits in the Sound panel header. Warmth and Brightness word sliders are the simple mode.
Grey zonesFrom split, octave, transpose, song key and controller range. Below the bottom note is hatched (“no notes”). Above the top note is lightly tinted (“tone of your notes”) and stays editable.
