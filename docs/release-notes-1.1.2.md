Worship Rig 1.1.2 makes soft playing quieter and hard playing louder on keyboards that felt flat, such as the Roland
FP-10. Nothing else changes: if you don't touch the two settings below, 1.1.2 sounds exactly like 1.1.1.

### Fit to my touch: playing harder gets louder again

Settings › Your keyboards › Fit to my touch used to put full volume at the middle of your "play hard" notes, so about
half of them were already as loud as it gets and digging in did nothing. Now full volume sits at your hardest notes,
so there is still room above your usual hard playing. Run Fit to my touch again to get the new fit; a curve you fitted
before stays as it is until you do.

### Range goes up to 6×

The Range slider (same place) now goes up to 6×, with more room on the quiet side:

- **Pianos** (sounds with several sample layers) span up to **60 dB** from the softest to the hardest note, up from
  30 dB at 3×. Past 3× only the soft end gets quieter; the hardest notes stay as loud as at 3×.
- **Other sampled sounds** already span 72 dB at 3× and stay there.
- 3× and below sound exactly as in 1.1.1.

Tip for Roland digital pianos: set the piano's own Key Touch to Light, then run Fit to my touch, then raise Range
until soft playing is as quiet as you want.

Worship Rig 2.0 reads the Range setting from a 1.1.2 backup; until 2.0 gets the wider range too, it plays a setting
above 3× at 3×.
